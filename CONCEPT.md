# HavenShield Digital Fortress Defenders — Core Concept

A nature-powered cyber-defense force battles corrupted digital invaders across a hybrid world where forests, fortresses, and virtual realms merge. Players become HavenShield Defenders, elite guardians who wield eco-tech armor, AI-enhanced weapons, and digital shields to protect the last uncorrupted stronghold: The Haven.

One line: A nature-powered cyber-defense shooter where elite HavenShield Defenders battle a rogue digital corruption across hybrid forest-cyber worlds.

## Halo-like, HavenShield-twisted
- Heroic defender role — last line of defense
- Epic sci-fi combat — armor, weapons, vehicles, large battles
- Enemy faction: The Corruption (rogue AI swarm)
- Maps: forest battlegrounds, digital glitch zones, fortress corridors, cyber-wild hybrids

## Initiative goals
- Protect HavenShield HQ
- Purge corrupted zones
- Reclaim digital-forest territories
- Prevent the rise of the Corruption Overmind

## Loadout
- Eco-tech armor
- Digital shields that convert corrupted code into power
- Bow-rifles, vine-launchers, pulse-roots
- Leaflight Drone companion
- Fortress building between missions

## Enemies
Glitch Wolves, Fractured Sentinels, Code Reapers, The Overmind.

## Visuals
Halo armor + Horizon nature + Tron energy. Green glow, leaf-tech plating, holographic shields.
